Ivey Alumni Events

Every event from the Ivey Alumni calendar, as a calendar you can subscribe to. It refreshes automatically every day.

Google Calendar

  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar.
  2. Next to "Other calendars", click + then From URL.
  3. Paste the link above and click Add calendar.

Outlook (including your Ivey email)

  1. In Outlook on the web, open Calendar and choose Add calendar.
  2. Pick Subscribe from web, paste the link, name it, and click Import.

iPhone / Apple Calendar

  1. Tap here to subscribe, or go to Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste the link.
